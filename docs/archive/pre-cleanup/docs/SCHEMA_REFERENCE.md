## Enum types

### display_state

Whether visitors may see a row: hidden (never), unlisted (direct link only), published, featured (promoted). Values: `hidden`, `unlisted`, `published`, `featured`.

### activation_state

Whether webhooks/sync jobs ingest data for a row: inactive (ignore), tracking (ingest), paused (keep history, stop ingesting). Values: `inactive`, `tracking`, `paused`.

### redaction_policy

How much of a repo's raw activity may surface publicly. Values: `none`, `messages_hidden`, `counts_only`, `curated_only`.

### project_status

Values: `idea`, `active`, `shipped`, `paused`, `archived`.

### signal_source

Where a signal (activity_events row) came from. Values: `github`, `vercel`, `admin`, `system`, `agent`.

### signal_visibility

Who may see a signal. Values: `public`, `private`, `redacted`.

### date_precision

How precisely a moment's date is known (pre-GitHub history is often month/year). Values: `day`, `month`, `year`.

### timeline_node_kind

Values: `milestone`, `release`, `progress`, `reflection`, `learning`, `event`.

### timeline_kind

Values: `global`, `project`, `evolution`, `custom`.

### media_kind

Values: `image`, `video`, `audio`, `model3d`, `document`, `other`.

### media_provider

Where the bytes live: Vercel Blob, an embed provider, one of John's deployed sites, or any external URL. Values: `blob`, `youtube`, `loom`, `deployment`, `external`.

### favorite_status

Values: `current`, `past`, `wishlist`.

### book_page_mode

Values: `generated`, `authored`, `hybrid`.

### screen_mode

Values: `iframe`, `video`, `feed`, `terminal`, `image`.

### entity_type

Polymorphic target type used by media_links, favorite_relations, shelf_slots, books, revisions, audit_log, approval_requests. Values: `project`, `repo`, `deployment`, `post`, `timeline_node`, `timeline`, `favorite`, `media`, `book`, `book_page`, `shelf`, `library_zone`, `activity_event`, `skill`, `client`, `ui_layout`.

### agent_message_role

Values: `system`, `user`, `assistant`, `tool`.

### approval_status

Values: `pending`, `approved`, `rejected`, `cancelled`.
