# Artwork and source credits

The illustrated entrance, hall, library, law reports and cabinet were produced with the built-in ImageGen tool for this portfolio. The first three use the federal architectural survey as a visual reference. The casebook and cabinet are editorial still lifes. The delivered WebPs are under [the local plate directory](../../public/travellers-rest/plates/), with an 800-pixel alternative for each view.

The interactive house was authored in Blender. The original monochrome archival likeness supplies the actual portrait texture and the neutral first frame. The 136-frame performance at 24 fps shifts gaze, turns the head, blinks and indicates the reports with the visible shoulder. It has no reconstructed hand or invented historical quotation. The website credits the movement as an animated interpretation. [Earl portrait catalog record](https://teva.contentdm.oclc.org/digital/collection/p15138coll33/id/285/) · [Architectural survey](https://www.loc.gov/item/tn0036/) · [Held sources and licenses](../../assets/travellers-rest/archive/README.md)
