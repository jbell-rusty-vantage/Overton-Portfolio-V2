# Archive evidence, attribution and rights

Letters, scans and historical cards identify their source records and distinguish transcription from interpretation. The HABS TN-14 survey, archival letters, maps and printed reports remain under their asset roots with credits and reproducible derivative preparation. Preserve source URLs and uncertain readings; never turn an imagined portrait reflection into archival quotation.

Historical-source rights and attribution live in assets/travellers-rest/archive and survey documentation. The short history sequence includes plantation wealth, slavery, war and emancipation; it must not silently erase that context. Runtime placements are in objects.ts, while the archive manifests own provenance. Public derivatives do not imply permission to publish every source master.
